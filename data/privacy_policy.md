# Livo 3D Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-31

**Last updated:** 2026-11-17

**Contact email:** hhyteam@zhongkuitech.cn

## 1. Overview

Livo 3D is a 3D character animation and augmented reality (AR) app for iPhone and iPad. No Livo 3D
account is required. Recordings, imported media, custom backgrounds and motion analysis are handled
on your device. We do not upload your recordings, selected videos or background photos to operate
these features.

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand feature usage and technical performance,
Apple StoreKit to manage purchases, and network services to deliver assets and community models.
These services receive information described below. Local processing of your creative content does
not mean that the App makes no network requests or collects no usage information.

## 2. Creative content and device permissions

### 2.1 Media you choose

You can select videos and photos through Apple's photo picker and import supported audio or model
files through the system file picker. The App receives the items you select; using a picker does
not give the App unrestricted access to your photo library or other files. Selected files may be
copied into the App's private storage so the requested feature can work after you reopen the App.

Custom background photos are processed on your device. The current App does not send these photos
to a cloud photo-to-3D generation service.

### 2.2 Camera and AR

When you choose an AR feature, the App requests camera access. ARKit uses the camera image and
spatial information, such as detected surfaces, to place a 3D model in your surroundings. This
processing takes place on your device. We do not upload AR camera frames or room maps to our
servers. You can revoke camera permission in iOS or iPadOS Settings; AR features will then be
unavailable.

### 2.3 Recordings and audio

Recording captures the App's rendered scene. **In AR mode, a recording may include the real-world
camera background**, including people or objects visible to your camera. Recording does not capture
other apps or your screen outside the rendered scene. The current recorder does not record your
microphone. Background music can be included from a selected audio track.

Finished recordings and generated exports are stored locally. They leave the App when you choose
to share or export them through a destination you select. That destination processes the shared
content under its own terms and privacy policy.

### 2.4 On-device motion analysis

Video Drive uses Google MediaPipe on your device to estimate body-pose landmarks from a video you
select. The selected video and the resulting landmarks are not uploaded for this analysis. The
landmarks drive the character during playback; we do not use them to identify you or build a
biometric identity profile.

## 3. Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes the monthly auto-renewing subscription and the one-time lifetime purchase through
StoreKit. Apple handles your Apple Account, payment method, billing and refund requests. We do not
receive your card number or payment credentials.

The App reads StoreKit product and transaction information to determine whether you have Pro
access. This includes the purchased product, transaction validity, subscription expiration and,
where provided by Apple, renewal, grace-period or revocation status. The App caches your plan and
expiration locally so it can recognise access between entitlement checks. It checks with StoreKit
when purchases are made or restored and when access is refreshed.

Purchase events and whether the App currently recognises you as a free or Pro user are also used
in the product analytics described below. Buying Pro does not disable analytics.

## 4. Usage analytics

The App includes **Google Analytics for Firebase**. It records usage events such as screens opened,
studio steps, selected character/dance/stage identifiers, music selections, recording and sharing
actions, asset-download results, and purchase/restore outcomes. Associated parameters can include
content identifiers or names, product identifiers, elapsed time and success/failure status. Some
names, such as the name of an imported music track, can be supplied by your files; they should not
contain information you do not want included in usage statistics.

Firebase also handles automatically collected app/device information and pseudonymous identifiers,
such as an app-instance identifier and, depending on SDK configuration and system availability, a
vendor identifier. App version, device/operating-system information, language, session activity and
approximate region may be associated with analytics. Pseudonymous identifiers are not the same as
anonymous data. Network service providers receive the IP address needed to serve a request; this
App does not request GPS/location permission for analytics.

We use this information to understand feature adoption, identify failed or slow operations, improve
performance across device types and measure purchase flows. The App does not display third-party
advertisements. We do not send the contents of your photos, videos, recordings or pose landmarks as
analytics events.

Read [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) for information about
Google's processing. The current App does not provide an in-app analytics opt-out switch. Contact
us about applicable privacy choices or rights; do not assume that deleting local recordings also
deletes analytics already processed by a service provider.

## 5. Asset delivery and community browsing

Characters, dances, music, stage images and thumbnails may be downloaded from our content storage
hosted on Supabase and its delivery infrastructure. These requests disclose the requested file,
your IP address and standard technical request information to the host. The App caches downloaded
assets locally. Creative media you import is not uploaded to this content host.

Community browsing uses Sketchfab. Search text, selected filters, model requests and standard
network information are sent to Sketchfab to return results. When you open an embedded 3D viewer,
the App loads third-party web content, which may use cookies or other web storage and collect
information under that provider's policy. Downloading a model contacts Sketchfab and/or its file
host. Community models remain subject to their authors' licences.

## 6. Service providers and disclosures

| Service | Purpose | Information involved |

We may also receive your email address and the information you choose to include if you contact
support. We use support messages to respond and resolve requests. Information may be disclosed
where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights and security. We do not sell your
creative content. Third-party services process information under their applicable terms and
privacy policies; this policy does not replace those policies.

## 7. Storage, retention and deletion

Local recordings, imported files, downloaded models, custom stages, thumbnails and settings remain
in the App's storage until deleted or cleared. Caches may be removed automatically. You can delete
recordings and custom stages using the App's available controls. Deleting the App removes its local
container; **offloading** the App may retain documents and data. Files you exported to Photos,
Files or another service, and device/cloud backups, are separate copies and must be managed in
those locations.

Purchase history is retained by Apple under its policies. Analytics and hosting information are
retained according to the relevant service's retention settings and policies; deleting the App
does not itself request deletion of previously transmitted information. Support correspondence is
kept as needed to resolve the request and satisfy applicable legal requirements. Contact us to
request information about retention or deletion; we will assess the request under applicable law
and the information we can reasonably identify.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You can choose which media to import, whether to enable camera access, whether to use community
web content and where to share exports. Camera permissions can be managed in device Settings.
Subscription cancellation is separate from data deletion: deleting the App does not cancel a
subscription. See the [Terms of Service](https://sites.google.com/view/livo3dtermsofservice) for subscription management.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of
personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact
**hhyteam@zhongkuitech.cn** to make a request. We may need information reasonably necessary to verify
and locate the relevant data. We do not maintain a Livo 3D user-account database, and pseudonymous
analytics records cannot always be matched to an email address. Where applicable, you may complain
to your local data-protection authority.

## 9. Security and international processing

The App uses iOS/iPadOS sandboxing for local storage and HTTPS for its network requests. No system
is completely secure. Protect your device and avoid including sensitive information in support
messages or files you share publicly.

Apple, Google, Supabase, Sketchfab and their infrastructure providers may process information in
countries other than your own. Their published privacy and data-processing terms explain their
processing arrangements and relevant safeguards.

## 10. Children's privacy

Livo 3D is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information
from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App,
contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Material changes will be communicated where required by applicable law.
