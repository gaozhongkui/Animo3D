# Livo 3D Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-31
**Last updated:** 2026-08-31
**Contact email:** hhyteam@zhongkuitech.cn

---

## 1. Summary (the short version)

Livo 3D is a 3D character animation and AR app. Everything you make stays on your device: the
videos you record are written to the app's own storage, and we operate no user accounts, no
analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Characters, dances and music are downloaded from our content server when you use them. Motion
analysis of a video you choose runs entirely on your device. The only content that ever leaves your
device is a photo you deliberately submit to the optional 3D character generation feature, and we
tell you before that happens.

---

## 2. Information the App handles

### 2.1 Content you provide

You may import a video from your photo library to drive a character's motion, import an audio file
as background music, and — in the optional character generation feature — select a photo. These are
chosen by you through the system picker; the App receives only the item you pick and never browses
your library.

### 2.2 Recordings you create

Recording captures the 3D scene the App renders. It does **not** capture your camera, your
microphone, your screen outside the App, or any other app. Finished clips are saved inside the
App's private storage and are only shared when you tap Share.

### 2.3 Motion analysis

When you drive a character with a video, the App analyses the body pose in that video using Google
MediaPipe, which runs **entirely on your device**. The video is never uploaded and the resulting
pose data is never stored or transmitted.

### 2.4 Optional 3D character generation

If you use the character generation feature, the photo you select **is uploaded** to Tripo3D
(tripo3d.ai) so a 3D model can be produced from it, and the resulting model is downloaded back to
your device. This is the only feature that sends your own content off the device, and it happens
only when you explicitly start a generation.

### 2.5 Downloaded assets

Characters, dance data, music and card images are fetched from our content server as you use them
and cached on your device. As with any web request, the hosting provider can see your IP address
and the file requested. We do not attach any identifier to these requests.

### 2.6 Purchase information

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment and receipts; we
only observe whether an active entitlement exists. We never see your payment details.

### 2.7 Information we do NOT collect

The App contains **no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs**. We do not collect or use device
identifiers, contacts, location, health data, browsing history, or your camera or microphone. We do
not require an account and we have no server that stores anything about you.

---

## 3. How your information is stored and processed

### 3.1 On your device

Recordings, imported music, downloaded assets, thumbnails and your settings are kept in the App's
private storage. Deleting the App removes all of it.

### 3.2 Content server

Asset files are served over HTTPS from our content host. Only static files are requested; no
personal data is sent.

### 3.3 Third-party processing

The optional character generation feature sends your selected photo to Tripo3D for processing, and
the 3D model browser queries the Sketchfab public API. Both are governed by their own privacy
policies.

---

## 4. Third-party services

| Service | Purpose | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| Apple StoreKit | In-app purchases and subscriptions | Handled by Apple; we see entitlement status only |
| Google MediaPipe | Body pose analysis | Runs on-device; nothing transmitted |
| Content host (asset delivery) | Downloading characters, dances, music | Standard web request; IP visible to the host |
| Tripo3D | Optional 3D character generation | The photo you choose to submit |
| Sketchfab | Optional 3D model browsing | Search queries you enter |

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties. Please review their policies.

---

## 5. Data retention and deletion

On-device data is retained until you delete it. You can delete an individual recording inside the
App, and deleting the App removes everything it stored, including cached assets. We hold no copy
of your content, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

---

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA to
access, correct, delete, or export your personal information. Because the App stores your content
only on your device and we operate no user database, you can exercise these rights directly on the
device. We do not sell or "share" your personal information as those terms are defined under U.S.
state privacy laws.

---

## 7. Security

The App relies on iOS sandboxing and device encryption to protect what it stores, and uses HTTPS
for all network requests. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

---

## 8. Children's privacy

Livo 3D is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information
from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the
address below.

---

## 9. International data transfers

If you use the optional character generation feature, the photo you submit may be processed on
servers outside your country. Asset downloads may likewise be served from infrastructure in another
country. Where required, appropriate safeguards apply.
