# Product Description

## Product Name
**Livo 3D**

Livo 3D: AI AR Dance Studio

---

## Core Positioning
A 3D creative dance studio integrating AI model generation, motion capture driving, and Augmented Reality (AR) technology.

---

## Key Features
1. **AI Character Generation:**
   - Instantly transform single images or text into high-quality 3D characters using Tripo AI technology.

2. **Multi-modal Motion Drive:**
   - Support real-time human pose tracking via camera or extracting motions from video files to drive 3D characters.

3. **AR Dance Space:**
   - Project your 3D characters into the real-world environment, breaking the fourth wall for an immersive dance performance.

---

## Brand Philosophy
**Livo** is a blend of **"Live"** and **"Vivid"**, aiming to convey the thrill of digital characters "coming to life" in the real world.

---

## Vision
**Let every creative spark dance.**
